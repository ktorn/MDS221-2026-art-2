title: Naive Babel Study
creators:
  - MDS221 Interactive Multimedia Installation (USJ Macau)
description: |
  Naive Babel Study is an interactive painterly tower piece inspired by the myth of Babel and geometric color-block composition. Four towers rise from a deep red ground, each floor a hand-painted block in earthy reds, blues, and ochres. Multilingual phrases—English, Chinese, Japanese, Italian, Russian, French, Portuguese, and Spanish—sit beside each layer, whispering how we build upward together.

  Click the canvas to focus, then use the keyboard:
  • S — add a square block to a randomly chosen tower (weighted toward centre towers)
  • R — add a wide horizontal rectangle block
  • P — save the current view as a PNG
  • F — toggle fullscreen

  Each tower holds up to eight floors; the eighth is always a triangle cap. When all four towers are complete (32 blocks total), the inscriptions scramble and pulse—a babel of symbols replacing the shared phrases.

  On load the scene breathes gently: an amber sun disc pulses behind the empty towers, inviting you to begin stacking. Block placement triggers a brief emanating ghost that expands and fades from the new piece.

  MDS221 – Interactive Multimedia Installation, University of Saint Joseph (USJ), Macau.
